The short version: SeniorPaw is a diary for your older dog or cat. It helps you keep track and talk to your vet, but it isn't a vet. Premium is a subscription you can cancel at any time.

Using SeniorPaw

You may use SeniorPaw on phones you own or control, for your own pets and the pets you look after. Please don't copy or resell the app, and don't try to break it or misuse the sharing service.

Not veterinary advice

  • SeniorPaw records what you log and can sum it up. It doesn't diagnose, treat or give medical advice. Summaries written by your phone's AI can be wrong.
  • Reminders help you remember. They don't replace your vet's instructions. A phone can hold back or delay reminders (for example to save battery), so don't rely on them alone for a dose that must not be missed.
  • If your pet is unwell, or it's an emergency, contact a vet straight away.

Your data

What you log is yours. It stays on your phone unless you share a pet; our privacy policy explains what happens then. Keep your phone's backup turned on: if you lose your phone or delete the app, we can't bring back pets you didn't share. You can save a copy at any time: Settings → Export all data.

Sharing a pet

Only invite people you trust. Everyone sharing a pet can see that pet's records and photos and add to them. Only add photos and notes you're happy for them to see. The owner can stop sharing at any time.
